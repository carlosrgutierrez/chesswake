# Attributions

Chess.com daily puzzles are obtained through its [Published-Data API](https://www.chess.com/news/view/published-data-api). Each puzzle shown during an alarm has a visible link to its original Chess.com page. ChessWake is independent and is not affiliated with Chess.com. The board palette, layout, sound, and piece artwork are not taken from Chess.com.

Chess piece SVGs in `src/assets/pieces/` are the [Chessnut pieces](https://github.com/LexLuengas/chessnut-pieces) by Alexis Luengas, distributed under the [Apache License 2.0](src/assets/pieces/LICENSE.txt). The SVG files were obtained from the [Lichess source repository](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/chessnut).
